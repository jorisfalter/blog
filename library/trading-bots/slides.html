Where it started

"We spotted the Maduro attack before it happened"

The dream

Insiders bet on Polymarket, and every bet is public. Spot them, and bet along.

Why I believed it

Source: @DidiTrading on X, 3 January 2026

Polymarket · insiders

Paris: someone tampered with a thermometer

The dream

My bot watches every Polymarket bet and flags the insiders.

Why I didn't win

April 2026: someone tampered with the temperature sensor at Paris airport and bet $65 on the exact temperature. It paid $27,500. My bot missed it: it only looked at big bets.

Source: Financial Times, 22 April 2026: French weather service alerts police after suspicious Polymarket bets · Bloomberg, 23 April 2026

Polymarket · insiders

Follow the wallets that know

The dream

A newsletter found wallets that bet big only on companies audited by KPMG, and keep winning. Follow them.

Why I didn't win

My bot never looked at those wallets. The signal was real: they kept winning. I just didn't catch it.

Source: Matt Lamers, EventWaves, 24 Feb 2026 (the author alleges no wrongdoing) · my own check of the wallets

Oil

Buy oil the moment an attack hits the news

The dream

Hear about an attack on oil supply first, and buy before the price jumps.

Why it didn't work

It did in 2026: oil went from $61 to $118 and stayed high for months. But that happens once every year or two, and on day one you can't tell if it's the real one.

Source: Brent crude prices 2025–2026 · my own study of oil spikes since 2015

Bitcoin

Buy Bitcoin when the US prints money

The dream

When the government pumps money in, Bitcoin goes up. Buy as soon as it happens.

Why it didn't work

It did, twice: when the Fed rescued the banks in 2023 and when the US bought back its bonds in 2026. Twice in three years, and on the day itself you have to guess the cause.

Source: idea after Reuters, 25 August 2026 · my own backtest, Binance 2023–2026

Stocks

Buy after great results

The dream

When a big tech stock jumps after great results, it keeps rising for days. Buy at the close, and ride it.

Why it didn't work

It only worked in the AI hype years. Sell the same day, the next day or ten days later: none of it held up in other years, or live.

Source: idea after De Tijd, 27 August 2026 (Nvidia +$350 billion in a day) · my own backtest and live alerts

Stocks

Copy the politicians

The dream

Members of Congress trade on what they know. Their trades are public. Copy them.

Why it didn't work

They report their trades weeks later. In my test, buying on the day you find out no longer beat the market.

Source: congressional trade disclosures via Capitol Trades · my own backtest

Polymarket

"Will Trump insult someone today?"

The dream

He does it almost every day. Bet yes, every day, and collect.

The catch

A yes already costs about 90¢. You win 10¢ a day, and lose 90¢ on the day he doesn't. That leaves a few cents a day.

Source: @CarOnPolymarket on X ("infinite money glitch"), April 2026
