Where Does The A380 Fly

What I built
A live map of every A380 route from flight data, with a page per airline, airport and country. Later extended to the 747, A350, 787, A340, 757 and 767. Node, MongoDB, Mapbox, on my own Hetzner server.
Result
About 7,000 visitors a month, mostly from Google. Google impressions went from ~1,300 to ~4,700 a day after the SEO work described in my SEO playbook.
